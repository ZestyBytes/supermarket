# Supermarket

## Platform
Mobile-first web app, also usable on desktop.

## Product and audience
A UK household chooses dinners, scales servings, excludes cupboard ingredients, reviews supermarket products and adds them to Tesco. Checkout always happens at Tesco. Open Supermarkets owns retailer integration. Hosting a static UI does not make the local Tesco API available remotely.

## Confirmed direction
User selected concept A, Fresh Market, on 9 September 2026: bright white surfaces, forest green accents, photographic meal grid, compact selectors, selected-meal strip, and Meals / Ingredients / Tesco navigation. Preserve the existing application functions. No full-plan basket writes during visual testing.
